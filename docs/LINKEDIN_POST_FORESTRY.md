## LinkedIn Post: Forest Inventory Intelligence

I have been extending my LiDAR and geospatial machine-learning work toward operational forestry and timberland decision support.

### Forest Inventory Intelligence | LiDAR + UAV + Multi-Sensor Analytics

The project asks a practical question:

**How can airborne LiDAR, UAV imagery, multispectral data, and field measurements be converted into repeatable forest-inventory products that support real management decisions?**

I developed a reproducible workflow that focuses on:

- LiDAR point-cloud and canopy-height processing
- DTM, DSM, and Canopy Height Model generation
- canopy-cover and forest-structure metrics
- candidate treetop and height-distribution analysis
- integration of multispectral vegetation information
- multi-temporal canopy-change analysis
- field-validation metrics including RMSE, MAE, bias, and R²
- spatial QA/QC for CRS, alignment, extent, nodata, and vertical assumptions
- GIS-ready decision layers for field verification and inventory prioritization

I also built a Python module that separates three important stages of production geospatial work: **data quality, quantitative validation, and decision support**.

The part I find most useful is moving beyond “making a LiDAR map.” The workflow is designed to answer a management question: **Where should field teams or inventory specialists focus additional attention, and why?**

Tools: **Python | NumPy | GIS | LiDAR | UAV | Remote Sensing | Spatial Analytics | Machine Learning**

The current repository documents the workflow transparently and distinguishes demonstrated analytical capability from field-validated results.
